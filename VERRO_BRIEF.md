## 3. The Eight Decisions

### D1 Category and Trust Mode
**Decision:** Light mode, clarity over drama  
**Reason:** Healthcare vertical - anxious buyers need polish as competence signal. Healthcare explicitly rules out dark mode per Verrolify.  
**Anxiety Level:** High (medical decisions are risk-averse)

### D2 One Pain, One Outcome
**Pain:** "Finding a credible hospital with clear information and easy contact"  
**Outcome:** "A trusted healthcare provider with modern facilities and straightforward inquiry process"  
**Single Promise:** KIMS Hospital provides clear, credible healthcare information with an easy path to inquiry

### D3 One CTA and Funnel Order
**CTA Verb + Channel:** "Inquire Now" via WhatsApp  
**Ordered Sections:**
1. Hero: Establish identity and primary CTA
2. Institutional Overview: Build credibility
3. Services: Answer "what do they offer?"
4. Facilities: Prove it's real with imagery
5. Consultation Journey: Show the process
6. Contact/Location: Answer "where and how?"
7. Closing CTA: Reinforce primary action

**Secondary CTAs:** Call, Map - available but not competing in hero

### D4 Visual World, 60-30-10 Colour
**Primary (60%):** Warm off-white canvas - `#F6F1E9` (heavyweight uncoated paper) - backgrounds and surfaces  
**Secondary (30%):** Charcoal - `#1F1F1F` (dark ink typography) - cards, nav, dividers  
**Accent (10%):** Restrained gold - `#C4A484` (aged brass) - CTAs, active states, emphasis moments  

**Real-world References:**
- Primary: Heavyweight uncoated paper stock
- Secondary: Dark ink on paper
- Accent: Aged brass hardware

**Canvas:** Never pure white or pure black, tinted toward hospital's material world (daylight, ceramic, steel)

### D5 Type System
**Display Face:** Fraunces (soft serif with warmth and authority) - headings at 800-900 weight  
**Body Face:** Source Sans 3 (quiet readable sans-serif) - body and UI  
**Display Size:** H1 minimum 64px (clamp for mobile), size jumps 3x between heading and body  
**Body Size:** 17-18px on mobile (older audiences), 16px minimum  
**Line Height:** 1.7-1.8 for body, 1.2-1.3 for headings  
**Measure:** 65-70 characters for body text  
**Letter Spacing:** Heading ~0.02em  
**Licence:** Google Fonts (OFL) - Fraunces, Source Sans 3

**Bilingual:** Not required for this fictional build (English only)

### D6 Motion Mode and Five-Part Hero
**Motion Intensity:** Whisper (Mode 1)  
**Hero Layout Structure:** Split-Screen  

**Five Parts:**
1. **Outcome Headline:** "Compassionate Care, Modern Medicine" (verbatim)
2. **Subheadline:** "KIMS Hospital delivers expert medical care with state-of-the-art facilities. Consult our specialists today." (20 words)
3. **Single CTA:** "Inquire Now" to WhatsApp deep link
4. **Visual Slot:** photo-1512678080530-7760d81faba6.avif - Hospital exterior establishing shot (1000x664 landscape)
5. **Trust Signal:** "A Verro Studio Fictional Build - For demonstration purposes only"

**Motion:** Reveal on below-fold blocks, Stagger on service grids (max 6), hover feedback. Nothing on LCP element. Reduced motion honoured.

### D7 Conversion Furniture (4-6 items)
1. **Authority Block** (Hero/Institutional): Hospital name, doctor name, establishment framing - Placed in hero and institutional overview
2. **Real Reviews** (N/A): Not applicable for fictional build - omitted
3. **Reciprocity Artifact** (Consultation): First-visit guide/checklist - "What to expect on your first visit" in consultation section
4. **Peak-End Confirmation** (Closing CTA): Clear next step with reassurance - "Ready to take the first step? Our team is here to help."
5. **Contrast Ordering** (Services): Service hierarchy with visual emphasis on most important offerings
6. **Smart Default** (Forms): Pre-filled WhatsApp message with context - "Hi, I'd like to inquire about services at KIMS Hospital"

### D8 Signature and Bans
**Signature Element:** Custom hospital building silhouette as subtle background texture in hero section - test: remove it, hero loses its KIMS-specific identity

**Ban List:**
- Em dashes in copy
- Banned words: leverage, seamless, empower, unlock, robust, actionable, data-driven, solutions, testament, landscape, delve, elevate
- "City's premier X" headlines
- Two competing CTAs in hero
- Stock words and generic medical jargon
- Gradient text
- Glassmorphism as decoration
- AI-generated illustrations
- Generic hospital corridor stock look
